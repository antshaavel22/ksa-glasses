Glasses and Lenses: Your Life's Hidden "Time Tax" and Real Cost

Most glasses and contact lens wearers think of them as cheap, simple daily solutions. However, when we add up all the costs and daily micro-tasks over the years, a completely different picture emerges. It is like a lifetime of renting an apartment — you pay endlessly, but you never own it.

Your Personal "Time Tax"

Have you ever calculated how much time you spend cleaning glasses, searching for lost frames, cleaning lenses, filling cases, and dealing with solutions?

  • Contact lens users spend an average of 8–12 minutes a day inserting, removing, and cleaning lenses.
  • Glasses wearers spend about 5–10 minutes a day cleaning lenses, dealing with fog, and adjusting frames.

If we take an average of 10 minutes per day, that adds up to 3,650 minutes a year — nearly 61 hours. Over 40 years (for example, between the ages of 25 and 65), this translates to exactly 101.4 full days spent solely on maintaining your vision aids. This is time you could use better or differently.

Health and Oxygen Deprivation

While glasses are safe for the eyes, contact lenses are not always harmless. A lens acts as a physical barrier on the eye, reducing the oxygen supply to the cornea (the front transparent layer of the eye) and causing hypoxia (oxygen deprivation). This frequently leads to dry eye syndrome, constant irritation, eye redness, and the ingrowth of blood vessels into the cornea. Getting rid of lenses restores the eye's natural oxygen supply and often helps eliminate chronic dryness.

Why Is Cut-Free Technology Gaining Popularity?

Traditional laser procedures like LASIK or FemtoLASIK require cutting the cornea to create a so-called flap, which is later folded back. Although these procedures are also fast and painless, the corneal flap carries a lifelong risk. If you engage in contact sports, diving, have small children, or lead an active lifestyle, a direct blow to the eye can displace this flap. The cut-free Flow3 laser procedure carries no such risk because the front surface of the eye is not cut, and no corneal flap is created.

What Is Flow3 SmartSurface?

Flow3 SmartSurface is a completely touchless surface procedure developed in collaboration with German laser manufacturer SCHWIND. It combines the LASEK, PRK, and TransPRK methods with intelligent SmartPulse technology, which ensures an unprecedentedly smooth corneal surface and faster healing.

How the Procedure Works Step-by-Step Without a Blade or Touch

  1. Local Anesthesia: Numbing eye drops are applied to your eye, making the procedure completely painless and eliminating the urge to blink.
  2. Epithelium Removal & Reshaping: Every eye is unique, and the doctor decides how the epithelium will be removed — either fully by laser or partially by the surgeon — to save energy. A lower energy load means more precise results, faster recovery, and greater safety. The SCHWIND AMARIS excimer laser gently removes the epithelium (the outermost, rapidly regenerating cell layer of the cornea) and immediately shapes the underlying corneal stroma to match your precise prescription.
  3. Speed That Matters: The laser is ultra-fast, operating at a frequency of 1050 Hz and correcting one diopter of nearsightedness in just 1.3 seconds. The entire laser process lasts less than 1 minute per eye, minimizing the risk of eye dryness and dehydration.
  4. Placement of Protective Lens: At the end of the procedure, a special oxygen-permeable silicone-hydrogel contact lens is placed on the eye. This acts like a protective bandage, allowing vision and comfort to feel good just minutes after the procedure.
  5. Natural Healing: The surface layer of the eye (epithelium) completely regrows under this protective lens within 3–5 days, after which the lens is removed at the eye clinic.
What is SmartPulse? Imagine sanding wood — standard lasers work like medium-grit sandpaper. SmartPulse technology, however, works like ultra-fine polishing paper. It positions laser pulses with microscopic density and precision, creating a demonstrably smoother corneal surface under a 1000x microscope. This smoothness is the secret to faster and more comfortable vision recovery.

When Is It Better to Choose the Comprehensive "Audit" Eye Exam?

At KSA Vision Clinic, no one is left halfway. If you do not fall into the Flow eligibility group, we have other very thorough diagnostic paths for you. You should opt for the comprehensive Audit exam:

  • If you are over 40 and need reading glasses: This is presbyopia (age-related farsightedness), caused by the natural stiffening of the eye's lens. Flow3 does not correct this, but KSA offers the comprehensive Audit vision exam to find alternative solutions.
  • If you are pregnant or breastfeeding: Hormonal changes can temporarily affect corneal shape and vision. We recommend postponing the exam and procedure until at least a couple of months after you have stopped breastfeeding.
  • If you have been diagnosed with serious eye conditions: Such as keratoconus (progressive thinning of the cornea), glaucoma (increased intraocular pressure), or cataracts. In these cases, laser procedures are contraindicated, but our doctors will help you find the appropriate treatment.

These are just a few reasons why it is worth undergoing the Audit exam. Most dangerous eye diseases develop stealthily over the years, and early diagnosis often helps prevent severe consequences.

10 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does the Flow3 procedure hurt?

No, the procedure itself is completely painless. Before starting, special numbing drops are placed in your eye. The laser only works on the surface of the cornea, so there is no mechanical cut or pressure on the eye. During the first couple of days after the procedure, the eye may be sensitive, watery, or sensitive to light, but this passes quickly as the surface layer of the eye regenerates.

2. What if I accidentally move my eye or blink during the laser treatment?

You do not need to worry about this. A special gentle eyelid holder keeps your eye open, making blinking mechanically impossible. You will feel like you can blink normally, but the eye remains open throughout the procedure. Additionally, our laser uses a unique 7D eye tracking system that tracks your eye movements in real time with extreme speed. If you make too large of a movement, the laser stops automatically in a fraction of a second.

3. How long do I need to be away from work (recovery time)?

Most Flow3 patients return to their normal pace of life and work in about one week. Because the surface layer of the cornea regenerates naturally, complete visual sharpening takes time, but functional vision (working on a computer, driving) is present within 5–6 days.

4. Are the results permanent, or can vision deteriorate again?

If your vision was stable before the procedure and you are between 18 and 45 years old, the results are generally permanent for life. In about 3% of cases — especially in those who initially had a very high minus prescription or underwent the procedure at a very young age (early 20s) — some regression (return of a minus prescription) may occur. In such cases, a touch-up procedure is performed.

5. Can the need for reading glasses after age 40 also be prevented with a laser?

No, unfortunately not. Age-related farsightedness (presbyopia) is a natural aging process of the lens inside the eye, which affects almost everyone, regardless of whether they have had laser correction on their cornea or not. At age 45–50, you may need reading glasses for close-up work, even if your distance vision remains perfect thanks to Flow3. In summary, it all depends on the elasticity of your body tissues; the more elastic the tissues, the later presbyopia develops.

6. I have very dry eyes, is Flow3 suitable for me?

Yes, Flow3 is often a much more suitable solution for dry eyes than traditional LASIK. Because Flow3 does not require cutting a corneal flap, the deep nerve fibers that regulate tear production are not damaged. Furthermore, the constant dryness and oxygen deprivation caused by wearing contact lenses will disappear.

7. What are the biggest restrictions immediately after the procedure?

During the first week, you must avoid rubbing your eyes, getting water in your eyes, going to the sauna, swimming, and using eye makeup. We also recommend wearing sunglasses in bright light when outdoors, as your eyes will be more sensitive to light. You can resume sports activities after one week.

8. How much does Flow3 cost, and can I pay in installments?

KSA offers flexible financing solutions and installment plans for all services. Our monthly payment is structured to be affordable on a daily basis — the investment in your vision is only about €18 per month more than what contact lens wearers constantly spend on new lenses and solutions.

9. What is included in the €69 suitability exam?

The suitability exam lasts 1 hour and includes extremely thorough diagnostics: corneal topography (mapping shape and thickness), visual acuity check, eye pressure measurement, and individual consultation with a doctor or optometrist. If you decide to undergo the procedure within 60 days, this fee is completely waived, as the exam cost is 100% deducted from the procedure price.

10. Can I go blind after the operation?

This is the most common fear, but with modern laser technology, it is practically impossible. The Flow3 laser only works on the surface layer of the cornea and does not touch the inner structures of the eye (such as the lens or retina).
